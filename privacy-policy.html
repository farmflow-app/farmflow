FarmFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

In short: FarmFlow stores your account details and the farm records you enter so the app can show them back to you. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and all of your farm data at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

FarmFlow is a poultry farm management app published by Reyne Flores Patalinghug ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at farmflow.support@gmail.com.

Information we collect

WhatWhy
Account details: your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's name and email address. If you sign in with Apple, we receive the name and email you choose to share (which may be an Apple private relay address). If you sign up with email, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us. To create your account, sign you in, verify your email, and greet you by name in the app.
Farm profile: the farm name and location you type in (we do not use your device's GPS). To label your farm in the app.
Farm records you enter: flocks (batch name, breed, house, bird count, age), egg collections, feed stock and consumption, mortality records, income and expenses, notes, and the activity history generated from these records. This is the core purpose of the app: to store your records and show you totals, trends and history.

FarmFlow does not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, health data, advertising identifiers, or browsing history. The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics.

How your information is stored

FarmFlow uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), a service provided by Google LLC, to sign you in and store your data securely in the cloud. Data is encrypted in transit. Security rules ensure that each account can only access its own farm's records. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy and security terms. If you use "Continue with Google", Google's Privacy Policy also applies to that sign-in. If you use "Continue with Apple", Apple's Privacy Policy applies to that sign-in.

How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features: recording and showing your farm records, totals and history.
  • To sign you in, verify your email address, and keep your account secure.
  • To respond to you if you contact us.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Your data is shared only with Google Firebase as our service provider, as described above, or when required by law. Each farm's records are private to the account (or accounts) linked to that farm.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and farm records for as long as your account exists, so they are there when you sign in.

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open More and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your sign-in account and your farm records (flocks, egg collections, feed, mortality, finance and activity history). This cannot be undone. You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing farmflow.support@gmail.com.

Your rights

You can view and correct your farm records directly in the app, and you can delete them or your whole account at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to how it is used. To make a request, contact us at farmflow.support@gmail.com.

Children

FarmFlow is intended for farm owners and workers and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
